About Golden Ice Cream

A neighbourhood freezer Accra trusts.

Golden Ice Cream started as one small shop on Dansoman Beach Road with a simple promise: never sell ice cream that has been allowed to melt.

Today we stock the full Frosty Bite range — vanilla, strawberry, chocolate and mixed tubs, sticks and cups — for families, resellers and event planners.

Our freezers run day and night, our stock turns over fast, and every carton leaves the shop solid. That's why our customers keep coming back — and why they bring their neighbours.
